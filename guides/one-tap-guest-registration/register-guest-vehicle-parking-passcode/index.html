How do I register a guest vehicle with a parking passcode?

Updated 2026-10-29

Ask your property management or leasing office for the guest parking passcode. Then, in your community's guest registration, choose your unit, enter the passcode, type the guest's license plate exactly as it appears on the car, and submit with a duration. Double-check the plate and note when the permit ends, so your guest is covered for the whole visit.

What you need before you start

Registering a guest takes four things. Only one of them can slow you down: the passcode.

  • Your parking passcode. It comes from your property management or leasing office. If you do not have one, ask them. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes.
  • Your unit. The permit is tied to your address, so know your unit before you begin.
  • The guest's license plate. Ask your guest to read it off the car, not from memory.
  • A duration in mind. Think about when the visit starts and how long it will last.

If you plan to use Metro Parking, you also need an iPhone, because the app is iPhone only. The site says it is coming to the App Store, so it is not listed there yet.

The order to enter things

The order matters because each step depends on the one before it. This is the flow in the Settings of Metro Parking.

One-time setup

  • Step 1 - Sign in with Apple. There is no password to create and no email to verify.
  • Step 2 - Choose your unit. Open Settings and pick your unit.
  • Step 3 - Enter your parking passcode. Type it exactly as your property gave it to you.
  • Step 4 - Add the vehicle's license plate. You can add an optional nickname so you can tell plates apart later.

Every time after

  • Step 5 - Tap Register and pick a duration. The permit is filed with your property's guest parking system.

Steps 1 to 4 happen once. After that, registering a guest means choosing a vehicle, a start time, a duration and a location. Your email and phone fill in automatically, so there is nothing else to type.

Mistakes first-timers make

  • Typing the plate wrong. One swapped letter and the permit no longer matches the car that is parked. Read the plate back from the car or a photo before you save it.
  • Guessing the passcode. If something does not go through, confirm the passcode with the leasing office instead of trying variations.
  • Picking a duration that is too short. Choose enough time to cover arrival, the visit and leaving.
  • Ignoring your community's limits. Some communities cap guest parking hours. Check your rules before you register a long stay.

After you register

Open the Usage tab to see all parking at your address. Each permit has a live status badge: active, upcoming or expired. If your guest's plans change, you can cancel a permit right from the list.

Metro Parking also shows month-to-date parking hours for each vehicle, so you can see where you stand against your community's limit. It does not block a registration that goes over that limit, so checking your community's rules is still up to you.

Expiration reminders and calendar alarms are part of Metro Parking Pro. They notify you before a permit ends, or drop an alarm at the exact expiration time.

What this works for and what it does not

Metro Parking files the permit with your property's guest parking system. It does not generate passcodes, and it cannot tell you whether your community is covered. If you are unsure, email support@vritam.com or see the Support page.

The first 3 registrations are free. Metro Parking Pro removes that limit. You can read more on the home page and in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Who gives me the parking passcode?

Your property management or leasing office. Metro Parking does not issue or generate passcodes, so ask them first.

Do I enter my unit and passcode every time I register a guest?

No. You add your unit, passcode and plates once in Settings. After that you choose a vehicle and a duration.

How many guests can I register for free?

The first 3 registrations are free. Metro Parking Pro removes that limit, and it is available monthly or yearly through the App Store.

Where is my passcode stored?

Passcodes are stored in the iOS Keychain. Delete Account in Settings erases your saved vehicles, contact details, passcode and parking history from your device and signs you out.

Can I cancel a guest permit after registering it?

Yes. Open the Usage tab and cancel the permit right from the list.

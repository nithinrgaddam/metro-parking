Why does the guest parking form ask for my phone and email every time?

Updated 2026-11-16

Guest parking forms ask for your phone and email every time because they are built as one-off forms that do not remember you. You can cut the repetition by turning on your phone's autofill or a password manager for contact fields and keeping your plates handy. The lasting fix is a tool that stores your contact details and plates once, so each registration only needs a vehicle and a duration.

Why the form keeps asking

Most guest parking portals are built around a single visit. Each registration is treated as a new form, so it asks for contact details again, even if you registered a guest yesterday. Often there is no account behind the form, so there is nothing to remember you with.

Contact details are usually collected so the property can reach you if something goes wrong with a permit. That is a fair reason to ask once. It is a poor reason to ask every single time.

The result is a form where the part you actually care about, which vehicle and for how long, is the shortest part of the job.

Quick fixes that help a little

  • Turn on contact autofill in your phone's settings, so your email and phone can be dropped in with a tap.
  • Use a password manager that fills in contact fields as well as logins.
  • Keep a note with your visitors' plates so you are not hunting for them while you are on the form.
  • Keep your parking passcode somewhere you can find it quickly.

These tricks have limits. Autofill only works when the form labels its fields in a way your phone recognizes, and many parking forms do not. You will often still type plates, unit and passcode by hand, and you still repeat every step on the next visit.

Save it once, then register in one tap

Metro Parking is an iPhone app built around exactly this annoyance. Your email and phone fill in automatically, and you save your license plates once. After that, a registration is just a vehicle and a duration. The app is coming to the App Store, so it is not listed yet.

Setup, done once

  • Sign in with Apple. There is no password to create and no email to verify.
  • In Settings, choose your unit and enter your parking passcode.
  • Save a plate. You can add a nickname if you like.

The passcode comes from your property management or leasing office. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes. If you do not have one yet, read where to find your guest parking passcode.

Every registration after that

  • Tap Register.
  • Choose a vehicle you saved, a start time, a duration and a location.
  • The permit is filed with your property's guest parking system.

No phone number, no email, no plate typing. For a walkthrough of the same flow, see how to register a guest vehicle with a parking passcode.

Keeping track after you register

If more than one visitor parks at your place, keep every plate on file. A nickname such as Mom's car makes the right choice obvious when you register.

The Usage tab shows all parking at your address. Each permit has a live status badge: active, upcoming or expired. You can cancel a permit from the list.

Monthly hours tracking shows month-to-date parking hours for each vehicle, so you can see where you stand against your community's limit. It shows you the hours. It does not block a registration that goes over.

Limits worth knowing

  • You need a parking passcode from your property management or leasing office.
  • Permits are filed with your property's guest parking system. If you are unsure whether your community's setup fits, email support@vritam.com or visit the support page before you rely on it.
  • It is an iPhone app only.
  • The first 3 registrations are free. Metro Parking Pro removes that limit and adds reminders and calendar alarms. It is billed monthly or yearly through the App Store, and you can cancel anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Your passcode is stored in the iOS Keychain. There are no ad SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps. Delete Account in Settings erases your saved vehicles, contact details, passcode and parking history from your device. More in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Not in Metro Parking. Your email and phone fill in automatically, so a registration is just a vehicle and a duration.

No. You save a plate once and pick it from your list at registration. Every plate you use can stay on file, each with an optional nickname.

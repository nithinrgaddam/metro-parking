How do I register guest parking ahead of time?

Updated 2026-11-17

Find out how your community takes guest registrations, usually a web portal or app that needs a passcode from your leasing office. Enter your unit, your guest's license plate, a future start date and time, and a duration. Save the confirmation. Check your community's rules on how far ahead you can register and how many hours each guest gets, because limits vary.

What to sort out before you register ahead

Registering early is the same as registering on the day, with one extra choice: when the permit should start. Get the basics ready first.

  • Your parking passcode. It comes from your property management or leasing office. If you do not have one yet, see where to find your guest parking passcode.
  • Your guest's license plate. Ask for it a day or two before the visit.
  • Arrival time and length of stay. Add a small buffer on both ends of the visit.
  • Your community's rules. Ask how far ahead you can register and how many hours a guest can park.

Rules differ from one community to the next, so the leasing office is the best source for limits. For a refresher on the process itself, read how guest parking registration works in apartments.

Why the start time matters

If you set a start time that is too early, the permit may begin before your guest arrives and use up hours you could have kept. Too late, and your guest is parked without a valid permit. Aim for their expected arrival, with a small cushion.

A future start time also gets the job off your plate. You register when you are thinking about it, not while your guest is circling the lot looking for a space.

How to schedule a guest permit in Metro Parking

Metro Parking is an iPhone app that is coming to the App Store. Setup is three steps, done once: sign in with Apple, add your unit and parking passcode in Settings, and save a plate. After that, registering is quick.

  • Tap Register.
  • Choose the vehicle. Every plate you have saved is on the list, each with an optional nickname.
  • Set the start time to when your guest will arrive, not to right now.
  • Pick a duration and a location.
  • Finish the registration. Your email and phone fill in automatically, so there is nothing to retype.

For the full walkthrough, see how to register a guest vehicle with a parking passcode. If you are tired of typing contact details into a form, this guide explains why forms ask every time.

How the upcoming badge works

Open the Usage tab. It lists all parking at your address, and every permit carries a live status badge: active, upcoming or expired.

A permit with a future start time sits in the list marked upcoming. When its start time arrives it shows as active, and once it ends it shows as expired. You can check your schedule at a glance instead of digging through old confirmations.

If plans change, cancel from the list

Your guest cancels, or the visit moves to another day. Find the permit in the Usage tab and cancel it right from the list. You do not need to leave the screen where you are already looking at it.

If the visit has only shifted, register again with the new start time. It is also worth glancing at the list before a busy weekend, because upcoming permits you no longer need are easy to spot.

Limits and things to keep in mind

  • You need a parking passcode from your property. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes.
  • Permits are filed with your property's guest parking system, so what is possible at your address depends on that system and your community's rules.
  • The first 3 registrations are free. After that, Metro Parking Pro gives unlimited registrations, plus expiration reminders and calendar alarms. It is billed monthly or yearly through the App Store.
  • Monthly hours tracking shows month-to-date parking hours for each vehicle, which helps you see where you stand against your community's limit. It does not block a registration that would go over.
  • The app is for iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead can I register guest parking?

That depends on your property's rules, so ask your leasing office. In Metro Parking you choose the start time when you register.

Will an advance permit show up before it starts?

Yes. In the Usage tab it appears with an upcoming badge until its start time, then shows as active.

Can I cancel a permit I registered ahead of time?

Yes. Open the Usage tab and cancel it right from the list if your plans change.

Do I need a passcode to register ahead?

Yes. Your property management or leasing office provides the parking passcode. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes.

Will I get a reminder before a permit expires?

Metro Parking Pro includes expiration reminders and calendar alarms. The first 3 registrations are free, and Pro removes that limit.

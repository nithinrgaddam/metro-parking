How do I plan guest parking for a week-long visit?

Updated 2026-11-22

Start by finding out how your community limits guest parking: hours per month, consecutive days, or both. Then add up the hours the car will be parked, up to 24 a day for seven days, plus any hours that vehicle has already used this month. If the total is close to the limit, ask management for an exception, shorten the stay, or move the car off-site for some days before the guest arrives.

Find out how your community counts guest parking

Before you do any math, learn the rule. Communities set guest limits in different ways. Some cap total hours per vehicle each month, some cap consecutive days, and some do both. Your leasing office or property management can tell you which applies.

Ask these questions before the visit is booked:

  • What is the monthly limit, and is it counted per vehicle?
  • Is there a cap on consecutive days, or a longest single permit?
  • Does the clock run only while a permit is active, or by calendar day?
  • How is a stay counted if it crosses into a new month?
  • Can you request an exception for a visit like this?

The same office provides the parking passcode used to register guest vehicles, so it is worth one conversation that covers all of it.

Do the hours math before the guest arrives

A week is 168 hours. If your community counts every hour a permit is active and the car stays parked day and night, that whole number lands on the limit.

  • Write down the arrival day and time and the departure day and time.
  • Count the hours the car will be parked between them. Overnight hours count too if permits run around the clock.
  • Add the hours that vehicle has already used this month.
  • Compare the total with the limit, and leave some slack in case the guest leaves late.

If the visit crosses the end of a month, do the math for each month separately, since a monthly limit usually starts over with the new month. Confirm that with your office rather than assuming it.

Also think about the cars themselves. If your guest brings two vehicles, each one has its own hours to account for.

Check the running total as the days add up

A plan on paper drifts once the visit starts. Guests arrive early, stay an extra evening, or run errands in the car. This is where Monthly Hours Tracking in Metro Parking helps. It shows month-to-date parking hours for each vehicle, so you can see where you stand against your community's limit on day three instead of finding out on day seven.

Register the stay and keep the end time in view

Setup is done once: sign in with Apple, add your unit and parking passcode in Settings, and save the guest's plate. After that, registering a stay looks like this:

  • Tap Register.
  • Choose the vehicle, a start time, a duration and a location.
  • Open the Usage tab to see each permit with a live badge: active, upcoming or expired.
  • Cancel a permit from that list if your guest leaves early.

Each registration counts toward your free allowance, and the first 3 are free. If you split a week into several shorter permits, you will use those up quickly. Metro Parking Pro removes the limit and includes expiration reminders and calendar alarms, which are handy on a long stay when the end time is easy to lose track of.

What to do if the week does not fit the limit

If your math lands over the limit, you have a few honest options:

  • Ask management for an exception in writing before the guest arrives.
  • Have the guest park somewhere else for part of the week.
  • Shorten the visit, or move some of the days into the next month if your community's rules allow it.

The limit is your community's call, and no app changes it. The most useful thing you can do is know your number early. For ways to stay under it, read How do I avoid going over my guest parking hour limit?

Frequently asked questions

How many hours is a week of guest parking?

A week is 168 hours if the car stays parked day and night. Your community's rules decide which hours count, so confirm that with the office before you plan around a number.

Should I register the whole week at once or day by day?

It depends on the longest duration your property's system allows and on your limit. One permit uses fewer registrations, while shorter permits give you more flexibility if plans change. In Metro Parking you choose a duration each time you register.

Can I see how many hours a car has used so far this month?

Yes. Monthly Hours Tracking in Metro Parking shows month-to-date parking hours for each vehicle, so you can compare it with your community's limit.

Will the app stop me from going over my community's limit?

No. It shows your hours, and Pro adds expiration reminders and calendar alarms. It does not block registrations or extend permits, so you decide how to stay within the limit.

What do I need from my property office to register a guest?

You need a parking passcode from your property management or leasing office. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes, so ask for yours before the visit.

What is a monthly guest parking hour limit?

Updated 2026-11-21

A monthly guest parking hour limit is a cap a community sets on the total hours guest vehicles can park on its property in a month. It is usually counted per vehicle or per unit, and it keeps visitor spaces free for real visitors. The exact number, what counts toward it and when it resets are set by your community, so confirm them with property management.

What the limit actually is

A monthly guest parking hour limit is a rule from your community that caps the total hours a guest can park in a month. It does not limit a single visit. It adds up all the time across visits.

Say a friend visits several times in one month. Each visit may be allowed on its own, but the hours stack up toward the cap. When the month rolls over, the count usually starts again, though the exact reset is up to your community.

Why communities set one

Visitor spaces are shared, and there are rarely many of them. A monthly cap keeps those spaces open for people who are really visiting. It also discourages a car from sitting there for weeks, and it gives management one simple rule to apply to everyone.

It is a community rule, not a universal one. The building next door may have a different limit, a different way of counting, or no limit at all.

What to confirm with property management

Before you rely on any number, ask your property management or leasing office. These are the questions worth asking:

  • How many guest hours are allowed per month?
  • Is the limit counted per guest vehicle, per unit, or per resident?
  • When does the count reset?
  • Does it count the hours you registered, or the time a car was actually parked?
  • What happens if you go over, and can extra hours be approved?

Get the answer in writing if you can. What management tells you is the rule that counts. Anything an app shows you is a convenience on top of it.

Habits that keep you under the cap

  • Match the duration to the visit instead of picking the longest option.
  • Check your month-to-date hours before you register a repeat visitor.
  • Tell regular guests that a cap exists, so they plan around it.
  • Leave a margin. Do not plan to use every last hour.
  • Ask management early if you expect a long stay, such as a visiting relative.

Frequently asked questions

Is the limit per guest or per resident?

It depends on your community. Some count hours per guest vehicle, others per unit, so ask property management how yours is counted.

Does Metro Parking stop me from going over my limit?

No. It shows month-to-date hours for each vehicle so you can see where you stand, but it does not enforce a limit or block a registration. Staying within the rule is up to you.

Does the count reset on the first of the month?

Many communities reset at the start of the calendar month, but yours may count differently. Confirm the reset date with your leasing office.

What should I do if I think I will exceed the limit?

Contact property management before the extra hours happen. Some communities may approve exceptions, but only they can tell you.

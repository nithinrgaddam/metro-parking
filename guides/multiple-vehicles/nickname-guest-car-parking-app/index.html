Can I nickname my guest's car in a parking app?

Updated 2026-11-23

Yes, in apps that let you save several vehicles, a nickname is a short label you attach to a stored license plate, like "Mom's Honda" or "Blue Subaru". It is optional, and it saves you from reading plate numbers aloud or guessing which one belongs to which guest. Nicknames matter most when you register guests often and have more than a couple of plates saved.

Why a nickname beats a plate number

A license plate is a string of letters and digits. It is easy to mix up, and you rarely memorize the plates of people you only host now and then.

A nickname turns that string into something you recognize at a glance. When you are standing in the hallway with a guest on the phone saying they are ten minutes out, you do not want to compare plates character by character.

Good nicknames are short and describe the person or the car:

  • The owner and the car, such as "Dad's truck" or "Priya's Civic".
  • The color and the make, such as "Red Mazda".
  • The role, such as "Cleaner" or "Dog walker", for people who visit on a schedule.

Skip anything that only makes sense to you next month. If you cannot tell who it is after a long gap, it is not a good label.

How nicknames work in Metro Parking

Metro Parking is an iPhone app for residents who register guest parking at their community. You save your plates once, and each saved plate can have an optional nickname. You can leave it blank if you only keep one or two plates on file.

The nickname does its job at registration time. You tap Register, pick the vehicle from your saved list, choose a duration, and the permit is filed with your property's guest parking system. With a nickname on each plate, you are choosing a name you know, not squinting at a plate.

Setup takes three steps, done once:

  • Sign in with Apple.
  • Add your unit and your parking passcode in Settings. The passcode comes from your property management or leasing office. Metro Parking does not issue it.
  • Save a plate, and add a nickname if you want one.

Keeping several plates on file

The more vehicles you save, the more a nickname helps. Metro Parking lets you keep every plate you use on file, so you are not retyping a plate each time a friend or relative shows up.

A few habits make a long list easy to live with:

  • Save a guest's plate the first time they visit. The second visit is then one tap.
  • Name each plate for the person, not the trip. "Sam's Prius" still works next spring.
  • Use a distinct nickname for each car, even if two guests drive the same make.
  • Remove plates you no longer need so the list stays short.

If you host family often, the same approach applies. See guest parking for frequent family visitors for how to set that up.

Checking what you registered

Picking the right car is one half of the job. The other half is knowing what is active. The Usage tab lists all parking at your address, and each permit shows a live badge: active, upcoming or expired. You can cancel a permit from that list if plans change.

The app also shows month-to-date parking hours for each vehicle, so you can see where you stand against your community's limit. It does not block a registration that goes over that limit, so keep an eye on the numbers.

If you are on Metro Parking Pro, you also get expiration reminders and optional calendar alarms. The first 3 registrations are free, and Pro removes that limit.

When a nickname is not worth the effort

If you only ever register one guest car, a nickname adds nothing. The plate itself is enough, and the field is optional for that reason.

It earns its place once you have three or more plates, or when several look alike on a small screen. Add it when you save the plate, since it takes a few seconds then and is easy to forget later.

Frequently asked questions

Is the nickname required when I save a plate?

No. Each saved plate can have an optional nickname, so you can leave it blank. It is most useful once you keep several plates on file.

Does the nickname show up on the permit?

Metro Parking uses the nickname to help you choose a vehicle in the app. The permit is filed with your property's guest parking system using the plate, so treat the nickname as a label for you.

Can I use a nickname for a guest I only host once?

You can. Many people skip it for one-off visitors and add it for guests who come back, since those are the plates you will pick again.

Do I need a parking passcode to use the app?

Yes. You get the passcode from your property management or leasing office, then enter it in Settings along with your unit. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes.

Can I delete a saved vehicle and its nickname?

Delete Account in Settings erases your saved vehicles, contact details, passcode and parking history from your device and signs you out. For details on how your data is handled, see the Privacy Policy.

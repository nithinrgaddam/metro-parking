What do active, upcoming and expired parking permits mean?

Updated 2026-11-07

An active parking permit is in force right now, so the vehicle can park legally. An upcoming permit has been registered but its start time has not arrived yet. An expired permit has passed its end time and no longer covers the vehicle. Only an active permit covers the car, so check the end time on anything you rely on.

What each permit status means

Guest parking permits move through three stages. Each stage answers one question: can this vehicle park here right now?

Active

An active permit is in force at this moment. Its start time has passed and its end time has not arrived. This is the only status that covers the vehicle.

Upcoming

An upcoming permit is registered but has not started. Think of a guest arriving tonight when you filed the permit this morning. Until the start time, it does not cover the vehicle.

Expired

An expired permit has passed its end time. It is a record of past parking, not current cover. If the guest is still parked, they need a new permit.

How a permit moves from upcoming to expired

Status follows the clock, not anything you tap. A permit registered for 6 pm shows as upcoming at noon, turns active at 6 pm, and turns expired when its duration runs out.

  • Upcoming becomes active when the start time arrives.
  • Active becomes expired when the end time arrives.
  • Expired is the final stage, so a guest who stays needs a new permit.

This is why a status is only as good as the moment you look at it. A permit that was active when your guest parked at breakfast may be expired by dinner.

How to read all parking at your address at a glance

Metro Parking is an iPhone app for registering guest parking at your community. Its Usage tab shows all parking at your address in one list, and every permit carries a live status badge: active, upcoming or expired.

A quick scan takes a few seconds:

  • Look for active badges first. These are the vehicles covered right now.
  • Check upcoming badges next. Confirm the start time matches when your guest actually arrives.
  • Glance at expired badges last. They show what has already ended, which helps when a guest says they had a permit.

Because the list covers your whole address, you see every permit side by side instead of checking one vehicle at a time.

What to do for each status

  • Active: note the end time. If your guest is staying longer, register another permit before the first one ends. Metro Parking Pro includes expiration reminders and calendar alarms, which can warn you ahead of time.
  • Upcoming: if plans change, cancel it right from the list. Here is how to cancel a guest parking permit.
  • Expired: nothing to do. If the guest is still on site, register again. With your plates saved, that is one tap.

The app also shows month-to-date parking hours for each vehicle, so you can see where you stand against your community's limit while you plan the next visit.

What to know before you rely on it

Registering needs a parking passcode from your property management or leasing office. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes. Once you add your unit and passcode in Settings and save a plate, permits are filed with your property's guest parking system.

Frequently asked questions

When does an upcoming permit become active?

At its start time. The badge is live, so it changes on its own once the start time passes. You do not need to tap anything.

Does an expired permit mean my guest got a violation?

No. Expired only means the permit's time window ended. Whether a violation follows depends on whether the vehicle stayed parked past that time and on your community's rules.

Can I cancel a permit I no longer need?

Yes. You can cancel a permit right from the list in the Usage tab. The steps are in the guide on cancelling a guest parking permit.

Is Metro Parking on the App Store yet?

It is listed as coming to the App Store, and it is for iPhone only. Check the home page for updates.

Who do I contact if a permit looks wrong?

Email support@vritam.com. Support is by email only, and you can find details on the support page.

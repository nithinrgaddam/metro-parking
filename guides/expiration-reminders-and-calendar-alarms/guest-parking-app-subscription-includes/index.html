What does a guest parking app subscription include?

Updated 2026-11-20

A guest parking app subscription usually lifts the cap on how many guest registrations you can make for free. It often adds convenience extras such as expiration reminders and calendar alarms. Billing is typically monthly or yearly through the App Store and renews automatically until you cancel. Prices differ from app to app, so check the subscription screen before you confirm.

What a guest parking subscription usually covers

Most guest parking apps work the same way. You get a small free allowance so you can try the app with real guests, then a subscription removes the limit.

  • Unlimited guest registrations instead of a fixed free count.
  • Extras that save you from watching the clock, such as a reminder before a permit expires.
  • Calendar alarms, for people who would rather have an alarm than a notification.
  • Billing handled by the App Store, monthly or yearly, renewing until you cancel.

What a subscription cannot do is change the rules at your property. You still need a parking passcode from your property management or leasing office, and your community's own limits still apply.

What Metro Parking Pro includes next to the free registrations

Metro Parking is an iPhone app that files your guest's permit with your property's guest parking system. It is coming to the App Store. Here is how the two tiers line up.

  • Free: your first 3 registrations.
  • Metro Parking Pro: removes the free registration limit and gives you unlimited registrations, reminders and calendar alarms.

The free part is a count, not a countdown. It is the first 3 registrations, not a time-limited trial, so nothing expires while you decide.

Reminders and calendar alarms come with Pro. If they are the reason you are considering a subscription, that is the feature to weigh.

How the reminders and calendar alarms work

  • Reminder: Metro Parking notifies you before a permit expires, so you have time to act.
  • Calendar alarm: it can drop an alarm at the exact expiration time, which suits people who prefer alarms.

Both are prompts, nothing more. They do not extend a permit, and they cannot make a guest move their car. They just make sure the expiration does not catch you by surprise.

Billing, prices and cancelling

Pro is available monthly or yearly. It renews automatically until you cancel it in your App Store settings. No prices are listed here, so look at the amount on the App Store subscription screen before you confirm.

To cancel at any time:

  • Open the iOS Settings app and tap your name.
  • Tap Subscriptions and select Metro Parking.
  • Choose cancel.

Your Pro features stay until the end of the current billing period. If anything about your subscription is unclear, write to support@vritam.com or visit the support page.

When Pro is worth it, and when the free registrations are enough

  • A few visitors a year: the first 3 free registrations may be all you need.
  • Guests who come often: unlimited registrations means you never hit a wall mid-month.
  • You have lost track of an expiration before: reminders and calendar alarms are built for exactly that.
  • You are not sure yet: set up first, then register a guest. Try it before you pay for it.

Setup is three steps, done once: sign in with Apple, add your unit and parking passcode, and save a plate. After that, you pick a vehicle and a duration. You can read more on the home page.

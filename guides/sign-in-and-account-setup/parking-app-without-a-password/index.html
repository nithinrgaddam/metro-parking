Can I use a parking app without a password?

Updated 2026-11-13

Yes. Some parking apps skip passwords entirely and use Sign in with Apple, which confirms you with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode instead. You create no password and verify no email address. Check the sign-in screen first, because apps that offer several sign-in options may still ask for a password. Expect a one-time setup afterward, such as adding your address and license plate.

Passwordless sign-in: what it means in practice

Sign in with Apple lets you use the Apple Account already on your iPhone. You approve with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, and the app never asks you to invent a password. There is nothing to forget, reuse or reset.

The catch is that many apps offer Sign in with Apple next to an email and password form. In that case you can skip the password, but the app still supports one. A truly passwordless app has Sign in with Apple as its only door.

Metro Parking is the second kind. It is an iPhone app for residents who register guest parking at their community, and Sign in with Apple is the only way in. There is no password to create and no email to verify. The app is coming to the App Store, so it is not yet listed.

A parking passcode is not a password

Going passwordless does not mean you need nothing else. To register a guest, the app needs the parking passcode for your property. That is a different thing from an account password.

  • The passcode comes from your property management or leasing office. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes.
  • You enter it once in Settings, along with your unit.
  • It is stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone.
  • Without it, the app has nothing to file your guest permits with.

If you do not have a passcode yet, ask your leasing office before you set anything up. Permits are filed with your property's guest parking system, so the passcode has to be the one your property gave you.

The one-time setup after you sign in

Setup is three steps, done once. After that, a guest registration is one tap.

  • Sign in with Apple. Approve with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.
  • Add your unit and your parking passcode. Open Settings, choose your unit and enter the passcode.
  • Save a plate. Add the vehicle's license plate. You can give each plate an optional nickname, and keep every plate you use on file.

When a guest arrives, tap Register, choose the vehicle, a start time, a duration and a location. Your email and phone fill in automatically, so you do not retype them each time.

Have these ready first

  • Your Apple Account signed in on your iPhone.
  • Your unit number.
  • The parking passcode from your leasing office.
  • The license plate of the first vehicle you want to register.

Privacy, free registrations and leaving

Metro Parking has no ad SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps. Signing in with Apple is the whole account story, so there is no password database of your own to worry about. You can read the details in the Privacy Policy.

The first 3 registrations are free. Metro Parking Pro removes that limit and adds unlimited registrations, reminders and calendar alarms. Pro is billed monthly or yearly through the App Store and renews until you cancel. To cancel, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select Metro Parking and cancel.

If you want to leave, use Delete Account in Settings. It erases your saved vehicles, contact details, passcode and parking history from your device and signs you out. Step-by-step help is in How do I delete my account in a parking app?. Metro Parking is not affiliated with Apple Inc.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to verify an email address?

No. Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in, so there is no email to verify and no confirmation link to wait for.

Can I sign in with Google or an email and password instead?

No. Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method in Metro Parking. If you would rather not use it, the app is not a fit for you.

Is the parking passcode the same as a password?

No. It is a code from your property management or leasing office that you enter once in Settings, next to your unit. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes.

Can I remove my account later?

Yes. Delete Account in Settings erases your saved vehicles, contact details, passcode and parking history from your device and signs you out.

Who do I contact if setup goes wrong?

Email support@vritam.com. You can also check the Support page for the in-app steps.

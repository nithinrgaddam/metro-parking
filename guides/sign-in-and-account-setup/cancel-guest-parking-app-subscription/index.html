Can I cancel a guest parking app subscription anytime?

Updated 2026-11-26

Yes. If the app bills through the App Store, you can cancel whenever you like, and you do it yourself in your iPhone's settings. Open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, pick the app and cancel. Canceling stops the next renewal, so you keep the paid features until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

How to cancel on your iPhone

When an app bills through the App Store, you cancel in iOS Settings, not inside the app. You do not have to email anyone or wait for a reply. It takes under a minute.

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  • Tap your name at the top of the screen.
  • Tap Subscriptions.
  • Pick the app from the list. For Metro Parking, select Metro Parking.
  • Tap the cancel option and confirm.

If the app is not in the list, you may be signed in to a different Apple account than the one that subscribed. Check the account at the top of Settings and try again.

What happens after you cancel

Canceling stops the next renewal. It does not end your plan today. With Metro Parking Pro, the Pro features stay until the end of the current billing period.

That means there is no need to cancel at the last minute out of fear of losing time you paid for. The Subscriptions screen shows your renewal date, so cancel any time before it.

It also means you can cancel early. If you only need Pro for one month, you can cancel soon after you subscribe and still keep the features until that period runs out.

What to know before you subscribe to Metro Parking

Metro Parking is an iPhone app for registering guest parking at your community. It is coming to the App Store, and subscriptions are billed and managed through the App Store, so the steps above are the ones you will use.

  • Try it before you pay for it. The first 3 registrations are free. This is not a time-limited trial. It is a count of registrations.
  • Pro removes the limit. Metro Parking Pro gives you unlimited registrations, plus expiration reminders and calendar alarms.
  • Monthly or yearly. Pro renews automatically until you cancel it.
  • Cancel anytime. Pro features remain until the end of the current billing period.

Check the price on the App Store subscription screen before you confirm. If your guests come rarely, monthly is the easier one to walk away from. If you have guests every week, yearly may suit you better.

Canceling is not the same as deleting your account

These are two separate steps. Your subscription lives with the App Store, so you cancel it in iOS Settings. Delete Account is in the app's own Settings. It erases your saved vehicles, contact details, passcode and parking history from your device, and signs you out.

If you are leaving for good, cancel the subscription first, then delete your account. The full walkthrough is in How do I delete my account in a parking app?.

Sign-in is Sign in with Apple only, so there is no password to reset or remember when you come back. More on that in Can I use a parking app without a password?.

Frequently asked questions

Do I lose Pro the moment I cancel?

No. Pro features remain until the end of the current billing period. Canceling only stops the subscription from renewing.

Does deleting the app cancel my subscription?

No. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store, so you need to cancel in iOS Settings under your name, then Subscriptions. Removing the app from your phone does not do that for you.

Will Metro Parking renew my subscription automatically?

Yes. Pro renews automatically, monthly or yearly, until you cancel it in your App Store settings.

Can I try Metro Parking before I pay?

Yes. The first 3 registrations are free, so you can see how it works at your address before you decide on Pro. It is a limit on registrations, not a countdown of days.

Who do I contact if I have a question about my account?

Email support@vritam.com. Support is by email only.

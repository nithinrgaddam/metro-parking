What it does

Everything you need to run guest parking at your address, and nothing you don't.

Register in seconds

Save your license plates once, then pick a start time, duration and location. Your email and phone fill in automatically.

See every active permit

The Usage tab shows all parking at your address with live status badges: active, upcoming or expired. Cancel a permit right from the list.

Never overstay

Get notified before a permit expires. Prefer alarms? Metro Parking can drop a calendar alarm at the exact expiration time.

Track your hours

See month-to-date parking hours for each vehicle, so you always know where you stand against your community's limit.

Private by design

Sign in with Apple, no passwords. Passcodes live in the iOS Keychain. No ad SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no tracking across apps.

Multiple vehicles

Keep every plate you use on file with an optional nickname, and pick the right one at registration time.

How it works

Three steps, once. After that it is one tap.

1

Sign in with Apple

No password to create, no email to verify.

2

Add your unit and passcode

Your parking passcode comes from your property management or leasing office. Metro Parking does not issue passcodes.

3

Save a plate, then register

Choose a vehicle and a duration, and the permit is filed with your property's guest parking system.
